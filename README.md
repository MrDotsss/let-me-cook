# LET ME COOK

> AI-powered recipe generation app that turns your ingredients, cravings, and custom instructions into recipes.

## FEATURES

- **Flexible recipe input**
  - Available ingredients
  - Taste or cravings
  - Custom instructions

- **Choose your Chef**
  - Select which AI model generates your recipe
  - Each model is personified as a different "Chef"

- **Save recipes**
  - Save generated recipes for later
  - Keep your favorite recipes in your collection

- **Share recipes**
  - Publish saved recipes for other users to discover

- **Community recipes**
  - Browse recipes shared by other users
  - Recipes that don't receive likes are eventually discarded

- **AI-generated recipes**
  - Generate recipes based on the user's provided ingredients, preferences, and instructions

## HOW IT WORKS

1. Tell **LET ME COOK** what you have, what you're craving, or what you want from your meal.
2. Choose your preferred **Chef** (AI model).
3. Let the Chef generate a recipe.
4. Save the recipe if you like it.
5. Share it with the community.
6. Browse recipes from other users and discover new meals.

## EXAMPLE INPUTS

### Ingredients

> Chicken breast, garlic, onion, soy sauce, rice

### Craving

> I want something spicy, savory, and comforting.

### Custom Instructions

> Make it high-protein and easy to cook in under 30 minutes.

These inputs can also be combined to give the Chef more context.

## PROJECT GOAL

LET ME COOK is a portfolio project focused on building an AI-powered application with user-generated content and community-driven recipe discovery.

It explores concepts such as:

- AI/LLM integration
- Prompt engineering
- Model selection
- User authentication
- Data persistence
- User-generated content
- Social interactions and likes
- Recipe discovery
